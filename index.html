About

Use AI to understand, not to shortcut

We teach BHS students how to use AI tools to study smarter, focusing on using AI to actually understand material, responsibly and within school rules.

01

Study guides

Turn a unit's notes into a clear guide, then check it for mistakes.

02

Quizzes and flashcards

Make practice tests before exams and learn to spot bad questions.

03

Organized classes

Keep each class's syllabus and notes in one place you can ask questions about.

04

Build things

Learn vibe coding and Claude Code, then build a learning website with your group.

Try it

Get a taste of the club

Three mini versions of real lessons. Pick one.

Summarizing your class notes

Great at condensing what you give it. Still check it didn't drop anything important.

Knowing today's news

Its knowledge stops at a cutoff date unless it can search the web. Check a news source.

Explaining a concept five different ways

One of its best uses. Ask for an analogy, a simpler version, or an example.

Long math with big numbers

It can slip on multi-step arithmetic. Use it to explain the method, and a calculator for the numbers.

Writing practice quiz questions

Fast and useful for studying. Just double-check the answer key.

Citing real sources

It can invent citations that look real. Always open the source yourself.

Main project

    
Build a learning website with your group

    
Groups of 2–3 design and build a website that helps students learn a topic from a BHS class, like a biology unit, a history period, or Spanish vocabulary. You'll use vibe coding and Claude Code and learn how websites work along the way. Every group presents at the June showcase.

    

      

        
Every site includes

        
    
          
  • A clear explanation of the topic, written and fact-checked by the group
    • 
          
  • At least one interactive feature: a quiz, flashcards, or a review game
    • 
          
  • A clean design that works on a phone
    • 
          
  • A "How we built it" page about what AI did and didn't do

    FAQ
    
    
    Questions people ask
    
    
    
      
    Do I need to know anything about AI or coding?
    No. We start from the basics, and plenty of members have never used AI for school before.
    
      
    Why does the meeting day keep changing?
    We meet during X block, which is part of Day 3 in the BHS six-day rotation. Since the rotation doesn't line up with the week, the weekday changes. Tap "Add meetings to my calendar" and every meeting shows up on your phone.
    
      
    What should I bring?
    A laptop if you have one, plus notes from a class you want to study for. No laptop? Pair up with someone.
    
      
    Is using AI for school allowed?
    It depends on the teacher, and that's our first ground rule: always follow each teacher's AI policy. We teach you to use AI to understand material, never to do your work for you.
    
      
    What if I miss a meeting?
    No problem. Ask in the WhatsApp group and an officer will catch you up. You can join at any point in the year.
    
      
    How do the gift cards work?
    We run giveaways and challenge prizes throughout the year. Come to meetings and watch the WhatsApp group for announcements.

    For members
    
    
    Ground rules
    
    
    Every member agrees to these. They keep the club useful and keep everyone out of trouble.
    
    
    
      
    Follow each teacher's AI rules
    When you're not sure what's allowed, ask the teacher.
    
      
    Learn, don't outsource
    Use AI to explain, quiz, and review. Never turn in AI-written work as your own.
    
      
    Always check
    Compare AI answers to your notes or textbook. AI can be confidently wrong.
    
      
    Protect privacy
    No other students' names, grades, or personal info in AI tools. Only upload class materials you're allowed to share.
